**WHMIS Regulations - What is a WHMIS Certificate?**     Some trainers and web sites offer a WHMIS certificate, what is it certifying? That is a good question as it may mean different things from different WHMIS training providers. The SafeAndCertified.com WHMIS certificate is certifying that on a specific day the named person completed a test and achieved the score indicated on the certificate. This is intended to be the documentation that that employee has achieved a passing grade on the test indicating that they successfully completed the test following the training.
