 **Can I get Personal WHMIS Training for Individuals?**     I am looking for a job and want to get my WHMIS certification to help me be more employable. This is another area where that may help you to get a job. It is still the Employers responsibility when you are hired to ensure that you are knowledgeable on WHMIS. If they are willing to accept your certificate that is up to them, but the responsibility is clearly with the Employer to ensure competence.
