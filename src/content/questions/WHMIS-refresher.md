# What is a WHMIS Refresher?
 
For most people, once they have learned something they will retain a good level of knowledge, especially if they are using that knowledge on an ongoing basis. So if you see a WHMIS symbol for compressed gas every day chances are pretty good you dont need retraining on that. But maybe you dont see any of the other WHMIS symbols. Some companies have their employees go through the training materials each time. If they know the material well it will not take very long. This can be followed up with a test to ensure a satisfactory level of knowledge.
